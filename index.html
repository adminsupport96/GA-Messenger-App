Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and store information received through our Facebook Page messaging integration.

1. Information We Collect

We receive and log the following information when a user sends a message to our Facebook Page:

  • Facebook display name
  • Message content (including any personal information shared within the message, such as phone numbers)
  • Timestamp of the message

2. How We Use This Information

The information collected is used solely for internal business purposes, including:

  • Logging and record-keeping of customer inquiries
  • Internal communication tracking
  • Business operations and customer follow-up

We do not use this information for marketing, advertising, or any third-party purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

All collected data is stored on a private, secured server with restricted access. Only authorized personnel have access to this data. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Data Retention

We retain message logs for a period of 12 months, after which data is deleted or anonymized unless required for ongoing business purposes.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. Information is only accessible to authorized internal staff.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we have collected from your messages. To make such a request, please contact us through our Facebook Page.

7. Facebook Platform

This integration operates through the Facebook Messenger Platform. Your use of Facebook is also governed by Facebook's Data Policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us via our Facebook Page.
